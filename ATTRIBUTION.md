## Machine Court — original artwork

The assets below were generated with OpenAI image generation for Greene Gaming's original courtroom supplement, then cropped, composited, or converted to WebP. The courtroom design and eight named court officials are original supplemental creations. No extracted published adventure images or maps are included in this asset set.

All 18 files are in `assets/courtroom/`. Scene and overlay retain matching 1672 × 941 dimensions; overlay preserves transparency. Portraits and cards are individual crops of the generated NPC sheet.
